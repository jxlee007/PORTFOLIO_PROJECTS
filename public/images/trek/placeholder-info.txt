Trek Page Image Requirements:
================================

The following images are referenced in the My Trek page and need to be placed in /public/images/:

1. my-trek-1.jpg - Hero image for main trek banner
2. cycling-neon.jpg - Favorite Sport section
3. motorsport-glow.jpg - Motorsports section
4. video-game-virtual.jpg - Video Games section
5. aesthetic-view.jpg - Aesthetics section
6. futuristic-dreams.jpg - Futuristic Dream section
7. cinematic-blade.jpg - Movies section (Blade Runner 2049 theme)
8. series-stranger.jpg - Favourite Series section (Stranger Things theme)

Design Notes:
- All images should have cinematic quality with high contrast
- Apply neon-red, blue, or purple color overlays/tints
- Images should be dramatic and evocative
- Recommended dimensions: 1920x1440px or similar high-res format
- Dark mode optimized with deep blacks and vibrant accents

Image placeholders can be created or real assets can be sourced from:
- Unsplash (https://unsplash.com)
- Pexels (https://pexels.com)
- User-uploaded assets
